# Changelog

## v2.0 (November 2026)

- **Choose your daily goal:** cards only, minutes only, or both thresholds together, on the Home screen or in Settings. The choice can be changed once a day; while locked, only to “Both” (switching to a single criterion needs a free phone).
- **Lower minimums:** 25 cards and 10 minutes (instead of 50 and 15). Goals you already set are kept.
- **Progress bar:** the floating circle at the top of the screen is now a horizontal bar (blue = cards, purple = minutes, orange = session, red = emergency). Tap it to open the app, drag it to move it.
- **About page redesigned:** gradient header with key numbers, “How it works” in 3 steps, features grouped by theme in cards that open on tap.
- **Jokers (streak rewards):** every week of streak (7 days in a row with the goal reached) earns a joker. A joker frees the phone for 30 minutes, even while locked or in a session — from Home or the block screen (two taps). Up to 10 in stock; a notification announces each new one.
- **Deck sessions:** choose a deck (or all decks), then a target — cards, minutes, or “Finish all” the deck's due cards. Only that deck's reviews count, and the session also ends as soon as the deck is finished.
- **Weekly summary:** every Sunday from 6 pm, a notification compares your week (cards, minutes, days reached, streak, jokers) with the previous one. Can be turned off in Settings → Reminders.
- **Uninstall protection removed → 3-minute reflection window:** uninstalling Ankineer Lock is always possible, but trying it first shows a big “think before uninstalling” window with a motivational message, your streak and a 3-minute timer. Play Store and App info are no longer blocked.
- **Tutorials (☰ → Tutorials):** all 26 videos of The Ankineer YouTube channel, with search and categories. Built into the app (still no Internet permission); each update that adds videos marks them NEW and sends a notification.
- **Deck picker:** only decks with due cards are listed, with a search field.
- Statistics, calendar, streaks, reminders and widgets follow the chosen goal.

## v1.0 — update (October 2026)

- **Uninstall protection:** still optional. Turning it off now waits a delay you choose — 1, 3, 7 or 30 days. While the protection is on, the delay can only be made longer (a pending stop is pushed back too), and changing the phone's clock doesn't shorten it.
- **“Unlock as soon as all due cards are done”** is now on by default (can be turned off in Settings → Due cards).
- **Automatic start** of the lock stays on by default.
- **Accessibility service turned off:** the warning now comes at every hourly check instead of every 3 hours, and also when Android switches the service off entirely. Setup step 3 has a clear warning explaining how to turn it back on.
- **About page redesigned:** one card with an icon per feature, and an **Ankineer ecosystem** grid (FocusForce, PopEdit, Ankineer AI) — each card opens its video.
- README: Ankineer ecosystem grid with video thumbnails.


## v1.0 — first public release (October 2026)

- Locks the phone until the daily AnkiDroid goal is reached: cards **and** minutes (minimum 50 cards · 15 min), PC reviews included after sync.
- Due-card options (sufficient / required), focus sessions, automatic start once setup is complete.
- Emergencies: code (once a day, 5 attempts) and 5-minute extreme emergency (once a day), protected against clock changes.
- Strict mode (Settings and uninstall blocked while locked) and optional “block uninstalling forever”.
- Success window, hourly reminders with quotes, alert when the service is stopped.
- Statistics: retroactive streaks, today's vs. overall retention, 7-day / 30-day / 1-year charts, activity heatmap, 365-day calendar.
- 4 home-screen widgets × 4 styles, floating progress circle.
- In-app update without uninstalling; copyable crash report; rescue mode.
- French / English, dark / light theme.
